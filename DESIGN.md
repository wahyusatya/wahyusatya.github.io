# Design System: Ash Research Lab & Systems Engineering

A dark-first, highly disciplined visual world prioritizing editorial clarity, dense technical credibility, and structural typography.

## Core Rules

1. **Restrained Color Discipline:** The interface is built on near-black tones (`#090a0d`, `#0d0f14`), muted slate surfaces (`#12151c`), and off-white text (`#f3f4f6`). A single cobalt accent (`#3b82f6`) is used strictly for active states, link indications, and status chips.
2. **Hairline Structural Architecture:** Thin, crisp 1px borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and subtle grid lines define sections. No heavy glowing halos, no bloated drop shadows, and no decorative glassmorphism.
3. **Typography Pairing:** Modern geometric sans-serif (Archivo / Inter) for crisp editorial hierarchy, paired with JetBrains Mono for system metrics, parameter badges, and architecture tags.
4. **Zero AI Cliché:** No generic AI brain visuals, no stock imagery, and no unnecessary 3D canvas overhead. The content leads through verified empirical research, clean systems architecture, and authentic credentials.
